Event 1

Summary Report: “AWS Community Day June 2026”

Event Objectives

  • Explore the latest trends in Cloud Computing and Generative AI.
  • Learn about modern AWS services and AI applications in enterprises.
  • Gain practical insights from AWS experts and the technology community.
  • Expand knowledge of AI Agents, Multi-Agent Systems, and cloud architecture.

Speakers

  • Tinh Truong – Speaker, AI & Context Engineering
  • Anh Pham – Speaker, Amazon Quick
  • Thinh Nguyen – Speaker, Amazon CloudFront
  • Team VIB – LotusHacks Hackathon Team
  • Duc Dao – Speaker, Large Language Models
  • Vy Lam – Speaker, Enterprise AI Systems

Key Highlights

Context Is Everything: Making AI Actually Work for You

  • AI performs effectively only when provided with sufficient context.
  • Introduced the concept of a Second AI Brain for long-term memory and personalized assistance.
  • Explained Context Engineering and Memory in AI Agent development.
  • Demonstrated the limitations of traditional Prompt Engineering.

Friendly AI Assistant with Amazon Quick

The session introduced Amazon Quick and its AI-powered data analytics capabilities.

  • Quick Chat Agent: Query data using natural language.
  • Quick Flows: Build automated workflows without coding.
  • Quick Spaces: Store and share organizational knowledge.
  • Quick Sight: Create AI-powered dashboards and business reports.

From Edge To Origin: CloudFront as Your Foundation

  • Explained the role of Amazon CloudFront as a global CDN.
  • Reduced latency and improved application performance.
  • Optimized costs through distributed caching.
  • Enhanced security using HTTPS and AWS WAF.
  • Improved system scalability and availability.

36 hrs with LotusHacks – Building UTMorpho from Idea to Reality

  • Shared the journey of developing a product within 36 hours during a Hackathon.
  • Demonstrated the MVP development process from idea to implementation.
  • Discussed teamwork under pressure and effective time management.
  • Showed how to solve real-world problems in a competitive environment.

Non-Determinism of “Deterministic” LLM Settings

  • Explained how Large Language Models generate the next token.
  • Clarified why outputs may still differ even with Temperature set to zero.
  • Discussed factors contributing to model non-determinism.
  • Introduced techniques to improve consistency in AI applications.

Enterprise-Grade Multi-Agent System: The Case of Startup Credit Scoring

  • Presented the architecture of Multi-Agent Systems.
  • Introduced the Virtual Credit Committee concept for credit assessment.
  • Explained how multiple AI agents collaborate to make decisions.
  • Highlighted enterprise deployment considerations and security requirements.

Key Takeaways

AI Mindset

  • Context Engineering: Context is often more important than prompt design.
  • Memory Systems: AI assistants can retain knowledge to provide long-term support.
  • Multi-Agent Systems: An emerging trend in enterprise AI applications.

Technical Architecture

  • Gained a deeper understanding of Amazon CloudFront for performance optimization and security.
  • Learned the architecture of AI Agents and Multi-Agent Systems.
  • Understood the causes of non-deterministic behavior in Large Language Models.
  • Explored AWS AI services for data analytics and intelligent applications.

Product Development Skills

  • Learned the importance of building products with an MVP mindset.
  • Improved understanding of teamwork and project time management.
  • Discovered practical ways to integrate AI into real-world business solutions.

Applying to Work

  • Explore using Amazon CloudFront to improve the performance and security of the ZeroBug Agent project.
  • Research and develop AI Agent and Multi-Agent applications for future projects.
  • Apply Context Engineering concepts to Generative AI applications.
  • Utilize Amazon Quick for AI-powered data analytics and dashboard creation.

Event Experience

Attending AWS Community Day June 2026 was an inspiring experience that broadened my understanding of Cloud Computing, Artificial Intelligence, and modern AWS technologies. The event provided valuable technical knowledge, practical experience, and opportunities to connect with experts and the AWS community.

Learning from Industry Experts

  • Speakers shared valuable real-world experiences in AI, cloud computing, and software development.
  • Practical case studies demonstrated how modern technologies are applied in enterprise environments.
  • The sessions also provided useful career insights for students and aspiring cloud engineers.

Exploring Emerging Technologies

  • Learned about Context Engineering, AI Agents, and Multi-Agent Systems.
  • Gained a better understanding of Amazon CloudFront and its role in improving application performance and security.
  • Explored Amazon Quick and its AI-powered capabilities for business intelligence and data analysis.

Networking and Community Engagement

  • Had the opportunity to meet AWS experts and technology enthusiasts.
  • Exchanged ideas and experiences with participants who share similar interests in Cloud and AI.
  • Expanded professional connections while learning about different real-world projects.

Lessons Learned

  • Multi-Agent Systems are becoming an important direction for enterprise AI development.
  • Amazon CloudFront enhances scalability, performance, and security for cloud applications.
  • Continuous learning and active participation in technical communities are essential for staying up to date with rapidly evolving technologies.
