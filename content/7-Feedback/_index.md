> Below are my personal experiences and feedback after participating in the **First Cloud AI Journey (FCAJ)** program. These comments are based on my actual experience throughout the internship, including project research, workshops, and technology events organized by the program.

### Overall Evaluation

**1. Learning and Working Environment**

The learning and working environment was friendly, professional, and open. Mentors, administrators, and other participants were always willing to provide support whenever I encountered difficulties during my internship. The collaborative atmosphere encouraged me to ask questions, share ideas, and learn from experienced professionals.

---

**2. Support from Mentors and Admin Team**

The mentors provided clear guidance on AWS, Cloud Computing, and AI-related topics whenever I needed assistance. Instead of giving direct answers, they encouraged me to explore different approaches and solve problems independently, which significantly improved my self-learning and analytical skills. The admin team also provided timely support with documents, schedules, and program activities, ensuring a smooth internship experience.

---

**3. Relevance Between the Internship and My Major**

The assigned tasks, including researching AWS services, writing technical blogs, participating in workshops, studying Serverless architecture, AI Agents, and preparing technical reports, were highly relevant to my Information Technology major. These activities allowed me to apply the knowledge gained at university while expanding my understanding of modern cloud technologies.

---

**4. Learning and Skill Development Opportunities**

Throughout the program, I had the opportunity to explore many modern technologies such as AWS Lambda, Amazon Bedrock, AWS Step Functions, AI Agents, and Serverless Computing. In addition to technical knowledge, I improved my research skills, technical writing, teamwork, time management, and presentation skills through various workshops and practical activities.

---

**5. Culture and Teamwork**

One of the aspects I appreciated most was the collaborative culture within the program. Everyone was willing to share knowledge, exchange ideas, and support each other whenever challenges arose. This created a positive learning environment and made me feel like an active member of the team despite being an intern.

---

**6. Opportunities Provided for Interns**

The program offered numerous opportunities to participate in workshops, seminars, and technology events organized by AWS and FCAJ. These activities helped me stay updated with the latest trends in Cloud Computing and Artificial Intelligence while expanding my professional network within the technology community.

---

### Additional Questions

**What was the most satisfying part of your internship?**

The most valuable part of my internship was the opportunity to work with modern Cloud Computing and AI technologies while participating in technical workshops and community events. The continuous support from mentors and the collaborative learning environment greatly boosted my confidence and motivation to learn.

---

**What do you think the program could improve?**

In my opinion, the program could include more hands-on laboratories and mini projects so that interns can gain additional practical experience. It would also be beneficial to allocate more time for direct discussions with mentors after each workshop to answer technical questions in greater depth.

---

**Would you recommend this internship program to your friends? Why?**

Yes. I would definitely recommend the **First Cloud AI Journey** program to anyone interested in Cloud Computing and Artificial Intelligence. It provides a professional learning environment, valuable hands-on experience, opportunities to learn from industry experts, and exposure to the latest AWS technologies.

---

### Suggestions and Expectations

- Organize more hands-on labs for practicing AWS services.
- Provide additional advanced workshops on AI Agents, Amazon Bedrock, and Serverless technologies.
- Increase the amount of mentoring and technical discussion sessions after workshops.
- Organize more networking and knowledge-sharing activities among interns from different cohorts.

I hope that the **First Cloud AI Journey** program will continue to grow by introducing more advanced topics in Cloud Computing and Artificial Intelligence, while providing even more practical learning opportunities for future participants.
