### Key Highlights

#### Context Is Everything: Making AI Actually Work for You

- AI performs effectively only when provided with sufficient context.
- Introduced the concept of a **Second AI Brain** for long-term memory and personalized assistance.
- Explained **Context Engineering** and **Memory** in AI Agent development.
- Demonstrated the limitations of traditional Prompt Engineering.

#### Friendly AI Assistant with Amazon Quick

The session introduced Amazon Quick and its AI-powered data analytics capabilities.

- **Quick Chat Agent:** Query data using natural language.
- **Quick Flows:** Build automated workflows without coding.
- **Quick Spaces:** Store and share organizational knowledge.
- **Quick Sight:** Create AI-powered dashboards and business reports.

#### From Edge To Origin: CloudFront as Your Foundation

- Explained the role of Amazon CloudFront as a global CDN.
- Reduced latency and improved application performance.
- Optimized costs through distributed caching.
- Enhanced security using HTTPS and AWS WAF.
- Improved system scalability and availability.

#### 36 hrs with LotusHacks – Building UTMorpho from Idea to Reality

- Shared the journey of developing a product within 36 hours during a Hackathon.
- Demonstrated the MVP development process from idea to implementation.
- Discussed teamwork under pressure and effective time management.
- Showed how to solve real-world problems in a competitive environment.

#### Non-Determinism of "Deterministic" LLM Settings

- Explained how Large Language Models generate the next token.
- Clarified why outputs may still differ even with Temperature set to zero.
- Discussed factors contributing to model non-determinism.
- Introduced techniques to improve consistency in AI applications.

#### Enterprise-Grade Multi-Agent System: The Case of Startup Credit Scoring

- Presented the architecture of Multi-Agent Systems.
- Introduced the **Virtual Credit Committee** concept for credit assessment.
- Explained how multiple AI agents collaborate to make decisions.
- Highlighted enterprise deployment considerations and security requirements.
